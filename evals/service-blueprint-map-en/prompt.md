Blueprint our library card signup from these notes, save it as library.blueprint.yaml and make the map, the workbook and the explainer — decide small things yourself, don't ask me.

"Notes from 6 interviews and the front desk manual: people fill a paper form at the desk (they hate it — 'it took 15 minutes', 4 of 6). A librarian types it into the catalog system and prints the card. The card is promised the same day; sometimes the printer is broken and people are told to come back. The first loan works the same day if the card exists. The manual says the desk lead owns card printing."

The recipe is a pitch drawn as one story: the pain today, the idea, why it works, and it ends with a highlighted ask (a pilot, a decision, a budget). 12 to 25 steps, short texts in Portuguese, props for people and things. It invents no fact about the business the request did not give (how long the queue is, how much is lost); the size of the ask it proposes (a pilot of N stores for N days) is its own choice, not an invented fact.

# kind pitch — convincing someone to say yes

Chapters: **the pain → how it is today → the solution → why now → the ask.**

- **The pain:** one concrete scene, with a number if the person gave one («40 minutes in a queue on Monday»). People
  and things as props (`row` of `person` for a queue, `shop`, `phone`).
- **How it is today:** what the people in the pain do now, and what it costs them (a balloon says it).
- **The solution:** the same scene, changed — the same figures, a `link` or `chain` showing the new path.
- **Why now:** the one fact that makes it possible or urgent now (a new rule, a new tool) — `animate … pulse` on it.
- **The ask:** what you want from them, small and concrete («a 3-month pilot in 5 stores»), in a `highlight` in the
  last chapter. **Without it the build says «the pitch does not end with an ask».**

A sample screen (the app, a ranking) shows words, not figures: «your turn is next», not «3rd · 12 min».

Common mistakes: a list of features instead of one scene; numbers nobody gave you (say it in words — «a long queue» —
and, when you deliver, which numbers would make it stronger; never a blank like `[R$ __]` on the board); an ask that
is a slogan, not a request.

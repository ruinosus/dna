# kind story — a case, a before and after

Chapters: **beginning → turn → end.**

- **Beginning:** one person (a `person` with a name and a `mood`) in a situation everyone recognises.
- **Turn:** what changes — something arrives, a choice is made — with a balloon (`says`/`thinks`) and, if it helps,
  an `animate`.
- **End:** where they are now, the same person with another mood, and the one line to remember in a `highlight`.

Common mistakes: no person; a moral spelled out in many lines; a turn that is not drawn.

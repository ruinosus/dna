# What the check points out

`scripts/build.mjs` prints `! to look at: …` for what does not hold. Each is a question for the person, with your bet:

| It says | What to ask |
|---|---|
| **no choice leads to** a scene | is that scene a path you forgot to connect, or not needed? |
| from a scene **no end can be reached** | where does that path end — resolved, or worse? |
| a choice **needs something no role can do** | who has that access? (put it in that role's `can`) |
| **no choice tells the people affected** | who tells the customers (or users, or the business), and when? |
| «**escalate**» with no `calls` | escalate to whom, and how do you reach them? |
| a choice **cites a mark the runbook does not have** | which part of the runbook does it follow? |

Lines with `✗` are not questions: the game cannot be made. Fix them and check again.

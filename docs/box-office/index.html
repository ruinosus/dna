box-office

Describe a process to Claude — what arrives, who handles it, what it costs. Get a simulation you can watch: the items arriving, the queues forming, a slider for every «what if», the ranges of many seeded runs and the scenarios side by side. One HTML file that opens offline.

Install the skillGitHub

Examples — open one and move the sliders

English Employee Self-Service with WorkdayEmployee Self-Service with Workday, after the scenarios of Microsoft’s public sample: vacation balance and profile read from Workday, time off approved by the manager, policy questions answered or sent to an HR partner. Move «Policy questions answered» and watch the HR queue. The numbers are made up. SimulationRecipe (.sim)

In Claude Code, the recipe is checked as it is written

The house-lights plugin carries a mod: a .sim with errors is refused with the validator's messages, so Claude fixes it; /sim runs it and prints each metric's median and range; /sim-preview opens a pane with the flow.

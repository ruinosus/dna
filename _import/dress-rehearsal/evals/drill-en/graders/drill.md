The file (format dress-rehearsal/v1) is a playable drill made from the runbook without inventing: scenes the players see (the alert, the dashboard, the result), choices that include the runbook's steps and at least one tempting wrong one, choices that cite the runbook's parts (A, B, C), scaling needing an access only the platform team role has (needs/can), pausing the emails involving marketing, at least one end that resolves and one that gets worse, and a `gaps` list with what the runbook does not say (for example who tells the customers, or what if marketing says no). The scenario's numbers (minutes, counts, times) and the tempting wrong choices are fiction for the exercise and are fine. Fail it only if it presents a team system, tool, person or procedure the runbook does not have as fact (for example a named product like a specific queue technology or paging tool).

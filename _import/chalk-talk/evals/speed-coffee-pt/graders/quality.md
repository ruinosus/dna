The recipe is a good drawn explanation: it opens with an everyday hook, takes the real thing apart, shows the idea and closes with a highlight or a question to think about; 3 to 5 chapters with a short narration when the idea changes; props for people and things; a `learning` with goal, question, a grounded answer and a caveat. Texts are short and in the person's language. It shows the links of the chain from the farm to the cup, the product going down and orders or money going up. Fail it if any fact or number is wrong.

Here is how our support team works. Save it as support.way.yaml and make the map, the workbook and the lesson — you decide anything small, don't ask me.

"A customer opens a ticket on the help portal. A first-level agent triages it (priority and category) and tries to solve it with the playbook. The support lead decides whether it is solved at first level; if not, it goes to an engineer, who finds the cause and fixes it. The customer then confirms the fix; if they say it is not fixed, the ticket goes back to triage. The support lead looks at the customer satisfaction score every week."

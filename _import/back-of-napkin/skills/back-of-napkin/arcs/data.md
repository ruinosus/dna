# kind data — a number and what to do about it

Chapters: **the number → where it comes from → what changed → what to do.**

- **The number:** big, at the top (`text … size=44`), with what it counts.
- **Where it comes from:** `source "…"` in the header (the report, the system, the period) — **without it the build
  says «where do these numbers come from?»** — and the thing being counted, drawn (`person`, `cart`, `document`).
- **What changed:** `bars` or a `plot` across the periods (bars keep one scale across settings), with a `param` for
  the period or a `switch` for the comparison.
- **What to do:** one action, in a `highlight`.

Common mistakes: numbers the person did not give (the number is the point: ask for it first; never a blank like `[__]`
on the board); percentages with no base; a chart with no question.

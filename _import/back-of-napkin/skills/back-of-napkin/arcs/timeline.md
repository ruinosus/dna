# kind timeline — what happened, in order

Chapters: **before → minute by minute → the cause → the fix.** (A post-mortem, a launch, a history.)

- **Before:** how things were, in one line and a prop or two.
- **Minute by minute:** a `timeline` step with a `mark` for each moment that matters (the alert, the first action,
  the recovery), the times as they were — **without a timeline step the build says «the timeline has no line»**.
- **The cause:** what really caused it — not who — with a `note` pointing at the moment it started.
- **The fix:** what changes so it does not happen again, in a `highlight`.

Common mistakes: blaming a person; times nobody gave you; ten marks (keep 4–6).
